Every engineering student knows the frustration of college infrastructure.

[PART 2: THE CORE IDEA]

GitNode treats the college not as one giant monolith database,
but as a network of autonomous GitHub repositories.

Every class, department, or student club becomes its own Node.

And the core principle is simple:
"Freedom at the Edge. Strictness at the Boundary."

1. The Public Commons (Public GitHub Repo):
   Hosted for free on GitHub Pages.
   This is where students build class websites, publish study notes,
   showcase projects, and collaborate through Pull Requests.

2. The Private Authority (Private GitHub Repo):
   This is where restricted institutional data lives.
   Attendance records, student rosters, and Role-Based Access rules (RBAC).

3. The Node.js Gateway:
   Sitting between the frontend and GitHub.
   It verifies who you are, validates data against strict JSON schemas,
   computes cryptographic SHA-256 hashes, and commits the data to GitHub.

[PART 3: REAL-WORLD COMPLIANCE — ADDRESSING THE "KAGAZRAAJ"]

Now, let me address the biggest real-world question:
"What about institutional compliance and paper audits?"

In Indian university administration, paper is the ultimate law.
The "Kagazraaj" demands physical registers with HOD signatures.

GitNode doesn't fight this reality—it embraces it.

During the month, zero paper is wasted.
Teachers and students interact digitally through the web console.
No manual percentage calculation by hand.

At the end of the month, GitNode aggregates the daily JSON commits
and automatically generates official, standardized PDF registers.

The HOD prints two summary pages, signs them, and files them.

We get a 90% reduction in paper usage, zero calculation errors,
and 100% compliance with university accreditation rules.
